Strategy and Events

We're the ones behind everything from ADS's biggest stage moments to the casual hangouts that make this club feel like home. Our department designs and delivers ADS's flagship initiative, from securing prominent guest speakers and managing end-to-end event logistics to building strategic, cross-campus collaborations. Beyond running the main stage, we shape the long-term roadmap of ADS, identifying high-impact opportunities that expand our reach and elevate the society’s presence across campus. We bring the ideas, forge the partnerships, and turn ambitious concepts into unforgettable experiences.
